What it changes on your computer

  • If you let it connect to Claude Code, it adds hooks to ~/.claude/settings.json and keeps a backup of the previous file next to it. You can switch this off in its menu or by uninstalling.
  • Lock-in mode, if you turn it on, closes browser tabs whose title matches its list of adult sites. A closed tab can usually be reopened with Ctrl+Shift+T, but don't rely on Lock-in mode around anything you can't afford to lose.
  • "Start with Windows" adds it to the programs that start when you sign in.
