claudetop on Android

For Android 8 and newer.

Claude Code runs on your PC, so the Android app is a companion: it follows claudetop on your PC over your Wi-Fi. The little Claude walks along the bottom of your phone's screen, over your other apps, and does what the one on your PC does. When Claude Code needs you or finishes while you're away from the PC, your phone gets a notification.

Download the APK

Install it

  1. Open the download on your phone. Android asks whether your browser (or file manager) may install apps: allow it for this once.
  2. Tap Install. Google Play Protect may warn that it doesn't know the app, because it isn't from the Play Store: tap More details → Install anyway.

Updates install the same way, over the old version, and keep your settings.

Pair it with your PC

  1. Make sure claudetop on your PC is version 0.9.0 or newer.
  2. On the PC, right-click the little Claude (or its tray icon) and pick Android phone → Pair a phone…. It shows a 6-digit code. If Windows asks about the firewall, allow claudetop on private networks.
  3. On the phone, open claudetop, tap Look for my PC, pick your PC and type in the code.

Phone and PC need to be on the same Wi-Fi. If the phone can't find the PC, tap Type in the address and enter the address the pairing window shows (like 192.168.1.20:47047).

The app asks for two permissions: notifications, and display over other apps so Claude can float over them. Without the second one, Claude only lives inside the app.

Using it

  • Tap Claude after it says "Done!" to tell it you've seen it.
  • Double-tap to pet it, hold to open the app, drag or fling it around.
  • Pick a hat in the app, and turn the 8-bit jingles on if you like them.
  • No notification comes when you're at the PC looking at Claude Code already.

Privacy

The phone talks straight to your PC over your own network; nothing goes through the internet. The PC shares what its Claude Code sessions are doing (working, needs you, done, the project folder's name) and whether its Claude Code window is in front. It never shares other windows' titles. Only paired phones get anything: to unpair one, use Android phone → (the phone) → Forget this phone on the PC, or Unpair in the app.

Something not working?

  • "No PC answered": check that claudetop runs on the PC with the pairing window open, that both are on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network), and that Windows' firewall allows claudetop on private networks.
  • Claude disappears after a while: some phones stop background apps to save battery. In Android's settings, set claudetop's battery use to Unrestricted.

Still stuck? Open an issue on GitHub.
